Ingredients:

1 cup of rice
1 cup of black-eyed peas or cowpeas
4–6 dried sorghum leaves (optional, for traditional colour)
4 cups of water
Salt to taste

Method of preparation:

Wash the beans and boil them in water until they begin to soften.
Add the washed sorghum leaves, if available, and continue boiling to release their colour.
Add the washed rice and salt to the pot.
Stir gently, cover, and cook over low heat until the rice and beans are both tender.
Remove the sorghum leaves before serving.
Serve hot with tomato stew, boiled eggs, fried plantains, spaghetti, or meat.

Note: Waakye is a popular Ghanaian dish made from rice and beans. The sorghum leaves traditionally give it a reddish-brown colour

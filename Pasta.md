 1. Place the Spaghetti in a pot of water and add a spoon full of salt.
2. Allow the pot to boil for 15-20 mins then drain the spaghetti.
3. Place another pot on the fire, and allow it to be hot, then pour some vegetable oil into it and allow to cook too
4. Add blended tomatoes, onions , pepper, carrot then salt and maggi. Allow it to cook
5. Add fish or meat if you have.
6. Add the drained spaghetti to the pot and mix very well.
7. Enjoy!

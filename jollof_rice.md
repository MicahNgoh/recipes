Ingredients:

2 cups of rice
4 fresh tomatoes
2 tablespoons of tomato paste
1 onion
2 cloves of garlic
1 teaspoon of ginger
1/2 cup of vegetable oil
2 cups of chicken stock or water
1 teaspoon of curry powder
1 teaspoon of thyme
Salt and pepper to taste

Method of preparation:

Blend the tomatoes, onion, garlic, and ginger into a smooth mixture.
Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and fry the tomato paste for about 5 minutes.
Add the blended mixture and cook for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Add the curry powder, thyme, salt, pepper, and chicken stock. Stir well.
Wash the rice and add it to the sauce.
Cover the pot and cook over low heat until the rice is soft and has absorbed the liquid. Add a little water if necessary.
Serve hot with fried chicken, fish, or vegetables.

1. Wash and fineky chop or grate the okra.
2. Boil the meat or fish with onions, salt and 
seasoning. 
3. Add the water and vegetable oil to the pot and boil.
 4. Add the okra, crayfish and pepper. 5. Stir well and cook for about 15mins until the okra 
is tender. 
6. Adjust the seasoning and allow the soup to simmer.
7. Serve with hot garri!!

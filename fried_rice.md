 1. Prepare your ingredients, tomato, carrot, onion, ginger, rice, pepper, and other spicies
2. Pour some oil into a warm dry pot already on the fire and allow it to cook abit.
3. Add some blended tomato, carrot , onion, green spices, ginger, pepper and allow them to cook.
4. Add some water to the resulting mixture, then add salt and maggi accordingly
5. Pour the rice into the water and mix, allow it to boil.
6. Enjoy!!

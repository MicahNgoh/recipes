 1. Wash the eru and waterleaf thouroughly.
2. Boil the meat and cow skin until tender.
3. Add the waterleaf and cook until it softens.
4. Add the shredded eru and stir thoroughly
5. Pour in Palm oil and add crayfish, pepper, salt and seasoning.
6. Allow everything to simmer, stirring ocasionally.
7. Serve with hot fufu!
